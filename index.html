Privacy & iPhone tips

Add to your Home Screen from Safari’s Share menu. After one online visit, the app works offline.

Only plain text is read from paste. Rich formatting and clipboard HTML are discarded; invisible control characters are removed. Text that looks like HTML stays literal text.

No notes are saved, uploaded, or included in app updates. Only app files are cached. Your browser, keyboard, and operating system remain outside this app’s control.

iOS may stop an app without sending a close event, so force-close detection and secure memory erasure cannot be guaranteed. The app starts empty and clears on visibility, page exit, and lost focus whenever those events arrive.

Copy puts text on the system clipboard. Clear only clears this editor; the app cannot guarantee clearing the system clipboard or its history. Clipboard access may require permission; if Paste is blocked, touch and hold in the editor and choose Paste.

Updates arrive automatically while online. An update may clear the editor.
